How the model works

Fasted PK curve: one-compartment oral absorption model (Bateman function), using a 3.1-hour mean time to maximum concentration and 3.0-hour mean plasma half-life reported for oral glycopyrrolate solution. The absorption-rate constant is fitted so the curve peaks at 3.1 hours.

High-fat condition: the model uses the reported fed timing (about 2.6-hour Tmax and 3.2-hour half-life) and scales peak amplitude to about 26% of the fasting peak, reflecting the observed reduction in Cmax. Reported overall exposure (AUC) was about 22% of fasting. This study used a specific high-fat meal, so it should not be generalized to every meal.

Sweat-control window: the timeline separately shows the published mean clinical onset (~2.3 hours) and mean duration of effect (~7.4 hours) from a hyperhidrosis study. GlycoView stops plotting activity after that mean duration so the graph is not mistaken for evidence of continued sweat control. Plasma concentration and sweat suppression are not the same thing.

Multiple doses: GlycoView can model up to six same-day doses separately. Each dose gets its own absorption/elimination curve, and the active curves are added together to create the full-day combined curve.

Percent shown at the top: the app simulates the current day, finds the highest modeled combined activity, and reports your current combined activity as a percentage of that day's predicted peak. Each dose card separately shows the current activity as a percentage of that dose's own modeled peak and whether it is building, rising, near peak, or past peak and declining.

Other / unknown food: there is not enough evidence to assign a defensible numeric multiplier for ordinary meals. GlycoView therefore displays the fasting reference timing but flags the result as uncertain instead of inventing a calorie-to-absorption formula.

Meal timing: oral glycopyrrolate is generally taken on an empty stomach. Product labeling for glycopyrrolate oral solution instructs taking it at least 1 hour before a meal or at least 2 hours after a meal, because high-fat food can substantially reduce oral bioavailability. Follow the directions for your own prescription and formulation.
